Daily Duas

Learn and recite authentic daily supplications from the Qur'an and Sunnah.

Morning

Morning Remembrance

اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا

Allahumma bika asbahna wa bika amsayna.

O Allah, by You we enter the morning and by You we enter the evening.

Reference: Abu Dawud

Sleep

Before Sleeping

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya.

In Your Name, O Allah, I die and I live.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari

Food

Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Bismillah.

In the Name of Allah.

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud
