What Are the Five Pillars?

The Five Pillars of Islam are fundamental obligations upon every Muslim. They strengthen faith, encourage good character, and establish a close relationship with Allah.

☝

Shahadah

Declaration of Faith

Testifying that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah and Muhammad ﷺ is His Messenger.

🕌

Salah

Prayer

Performing the five daily prayers at their prescribed times with sincerity and devotion.

💰

Zakat

Charity

Giving a portion of one's wealth to help the poor and needy, purifying both wealth and heart.

🌙

Sawm

Fasting

Fasting during the blessed month of Ramadan from dawn until sunset to attain taqwa.

Hajj

Pilgrimage

Pilgrimage to the Sacred House in Makkah at least once in a lifetime if physically and financially able.
