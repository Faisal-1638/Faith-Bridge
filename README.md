# Faith-Bridge

Connecting hearts with faith and minds with knowledge.

Welcome to **Faith-Bridge** —a simple Islamic website created to help Muslims learn and understand the basic and fundamental teachings of Islam in an accessible and user-friendly way.

The goal of this project is to make beneficial Islamic knowledge easier to explore and to provide useful digital tools that Muslims can use in their daily lives.

## Our Purpose

Islam is a way of life, and learning its foundations is important for every Muslim.

Faith-Bridge is designed as a starting point for anyone who wants to learn more about Islam — whether they are beginning their journey, revising the basics, or simply looking for useful Islamic resources.

The project aims to:

Make basic Islamic knowledge easier to learn
Help Muslims understand the fundamental teachings of Islam
Provide useful resources for everyday worship
Encourage Muslims to remember Allah and strengthen their faith
Make Islamic learning simple and accessible to everyone

## Features

* **Five Pillars of Islam:** Clear and easy-to-read guides explaining the core foundations of faith. Includes Iman, Salah, Jakah, Hajj, Ramadan.

* **Duas:** Find useful Duas (supplications) that can be remembered and used in everyday life.

* **Tasbih:** Use the digital Tasbih counter to help with Dhikr and remembrance of Allah.

* **Quran:** Explore the Holy Quran and spend time with the words of Allah.

* **Islamic Time:** Access Islamic time-related information through the website's time section.

* **Clean & Responsive UI:** Designed to be comfortable to read across mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers.
